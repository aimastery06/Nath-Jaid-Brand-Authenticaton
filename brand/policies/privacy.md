## What we collect

- **When you place an order:** your name, email address, shipping address, and the candles you bought. Payment details are entered on Stripe's secure checkout and never reach our servers.
- **When you create an account:** your name, email address, and a securely stored password.
- **In your browser:** your bag is kept on your own device. We use essential cookies only to keep you signed in.

## Why we collect it

To process, ship, and support your orders; to let you see your order history; and to keep your account secure. We do not sell your information.

## Who helps us

- **Stripe** processes payments.
- **Supabase** stores accounts and orders.
- **Vercel** hosts this website.

Each handles your information only to provide its service to us.

## Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your information at any time by writing to [info@nathjaid.com](mailto:info@nathjaid.com) or by requesting account deletion from your account page. We keep order records for as long as the law requires.
