## Returns

You can return unused, unburned candles in their original condition within 30 days of delivery. Email [info@nathjaid.com](mailto:info@nathjaid.com) with your order number and we'll reply with return instructions.

For safety and hygiene, we can't accept candles that have been lit.

## Damaged or incorrect orders

If a candle arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, write to us within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We'll replace it or refund it — no return needed.

## Refunds

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank may take 5–10 business days to show it. Original shipping charges are refunded only when the return is due to our error.

## Cancelling an order

If you'd like to cancel, email us as soon as possible. We can cancel any order that hasn't shipped yet and refund it in full.
