## Where we ship

We currently ship within the United States.

## What it costs

Shipping is complimentary on orders of $35 or more. Orders under $35 ship for a flat $6.

## When your order leaves us

Orders ship within 2–3 business days. Once your candles are on their way, you'll find the carrier and tracking number in your order details. If you checked out as a guest, write to us and we'll send them along.

## Something wrong with your delivery?

If a candle arrives damaged, email [info@nathjaid.com](mailto:info@nathjaid.com) within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo, and we'll make it right.
