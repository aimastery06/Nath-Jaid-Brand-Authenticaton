# NATH & JAID Fall Collection Image Prompts

These assets were created with the built-in image-generation workflow. The prompts below preserve the final visual system for future variants.

## Shared Product Prompt

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: high-quality ecommerce product-card and product-detail image for the NATH & JAID candle website
Primary request: Create a photorealistic premium studio photograph of the [PRODUCT NAME] candle from the NATH & JAID Fall Collection.
Scene/backdrop: seamless warm-cream studio backdrop and pale matte travertine pedestal, with only the restrained scent-specific props listed below.
Subject: one 14.5 oz three-wick candle in a straight-sided translucent [VESSEL COLOR] glass vessel, creamy soy-blend wax, three clean unlit cotton wicks, refined cream paper label with a thin evergreen border.
Style/medium: premium ecommerce product photography, realistic materials and natural imperfections, sharp product edges, believable label application.
Composition/framing: vertical 4:5 portrait, candle centered, full vessel visible, slight three-quarter eye-level camera angle, generous breathing room.
Lighting/mood: soft directional window-style studio light from upper left, subtle shadow, warm and inviting but color accurate.
Color palette: Warm Cream #F7F2E8, Evergreen #183F35, Burnt Terracotta #B65F43, Deep Amber #A56A32, Warm Gold #C79A4A, Charcoal #292824.
Text centered on label: “NATH & JAID”; “[PRODUCT NAME]”; smaller “3-WICK CANDLE” and “14.5 OZ / 411 G”.
Constraints: exact brand and product spelling; editorial serif product name and clean sans serif details; identical vessel shape, label proportions, scale, camera, backdrop, and lighting across every product; each scent must keep its assigned glass color so the collection is easy to distinguish.
Avoid: people, hands, flames, smoke, extra candles, excessive props, busy patterns, gradients, discount graphics, warped glass, misspelled text, fake logos, and watermarks.
```

## Fall Collection Hero Prompt

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: high-quality wide homepage hero for the NATH & JAID Fall Collection
Input images: Use the four approved v2 product portraits as strict references for vessel shape, distinct vessel colors, label, typography, wax, and product spelling.
Primary request: Create a photorealistic editorial group photograph featuring all four candles in a refined autumn home setting.
Scene/backdrop: warm cream plaster wall, pale travertine console, soft linen curtain, and a restrained arrangement of one dried maple leaf, cinnamon stick, apple slice, vanilla pod, halved fig, and short cedar sprig.
Subject: all four matching candle forms in their assigned glass colors—burnt terracotta, honey gold, champagne, and deep evergreen. Labels face camera and remain legible. Vanilla Amber sits slightly forward and is lit with three small realistic flames; the other candles remain unlit.
Style/medium: premium editorial home-fragrance campaign photography with tactile materials, believable glass reflections, and realistic candle flames.
Composition/framing: wide 16:9 landscape; group the products across the right 55% and preserve the left 40% as clean warm-cream negative space for an HTML headline and CTA.
Lighting/mood: golden late-afternoon window light from left, soft long shadows, calm and understated fall atmosphere.
Text: no added headline or promotional copy. Keep only the label text from the approved product references.
Constraints: preserve vessel shape, label proportions, brand spelling, and exact product names; all four products must remain distinct and identifiable.
Avoid: people, hands, extra or duplicated candles, pumpkins, holiday ornaments, crowded styling, heavy orange cast, distorted glass, incorrect labels, added logos, and watermarks.
```
