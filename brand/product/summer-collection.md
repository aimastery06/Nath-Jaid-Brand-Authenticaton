## Citrus Grove

- **Slug:** `citrus-grove`
- **Primary image:** `images/summer/citrus-grove-01.webp`
- **Vessel:** translucent sunwashed gold
- **Scent mood:** Bright & Citrusy
- **Short notes:** Mandarin · Bergamot · Orange Blossom
- **Scent notes:** ripe mandarin, sparkling bergamot, orange blossom
- **Note structure:** Top — Mandarin; Heart — Bergamot; Base — Orange Blossom
- **Mood:** Bright, fresh, and optimistic.
- **Description:** Ripe mandarin and sparkling bergamot soften into orange blossom for a fragrance filled with clean summer light.
- **Product story:** Made for opening the windows early and letting the whole day in.

- **Scent notes:** coconut milk, sun-warmed linen, white musk
- **Note structure:** Top — Coconut Milk; Heart — Sun-Warmed Linen; Base — White Musk
