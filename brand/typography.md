# NATH & JAID Typography

The type system should feel editorial and premium while remaining highly readable.

## Preferred Pairing

### Headings — DM Serif Display

Use for:

- hero headlines
- page titles
- collection titles
- editorial section headings
- selected product names

Headings should feel expressive, calm, and spacious. Avoid excessive all-caps styling.

### Body and UI — Inter

Use for:

- body copy
- navigation
- buttons
- labels
- prices
- forms
- product details

Body copy must remain easy to read on small screens.

## Approved Alternatives

If the preferred fonts are unavailable:

- Heading alternatives: Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, Libre Baskerville
- Body alternatives: Manrope, DM Sans, Geist

Use one serif family and one sans-serif family only. Do not introduce decorative typefaces.

## Implementation Guidance

- Load only the weights actually used.
- Use responsive font sizing with a restrained scale.
- Keep line lengths comfortable and line height generous.
- Preserve logical heading levels in the document structure.
- Do not sacrifice contrast or readability for visual style.
