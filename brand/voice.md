# NATH & JAID Voice

## How We Sound

The brand voice is:

- **Warm** — speak like someone inviting a customer into a beautifully scented room.
- **Simple** — avoid unnecessary marketing language.
- **Sensory** — help customers imagine the fragrance and atmosphere.
- **Confident** — state the value clearly without overselling.
- **Emotional** — describe the mood a scent creates, not only its ingredients.

Use concise copy. Premium consumer brands often communicate with fewer words.

## Primary Tagline

> **Light the moment.**

Use this as the main brand line. Do not overcrowd a page with multiple taglines.

## Campaign Language

- A scent for every season.
- Make the room feel different.
- Fragrance for the moments you want to keep.
- Fall Collection: **The season for staying in.**

## Copy Examples

Avoid:

> Experience the ultimate luxury candle made from premium ingredients.

Prefer:

> Creamy vanilla, golden amber, and soft sandalwood — made for slow evenings at home.

Avoid:

> Our innovative candles provide an incredible aromatic experience.

Prefer:

> Light it when the evening starts to slow down.

Avoid:

> Buy our new fall candle collection today!

Prefer:

> Fall has arrived. Meet four scents made for cooler nights, warm drinks, and staying in.

## Writing Rules

- Use realistic NATH & JAID copy; never use lorem ipsum.
- Favor short, evocative sentences over long sales copy.
- Describe rooms, rituals, seasons, memories, and moods.
- Avoid hype, superlatives, corporate language, and aggressive sales pressure.
- Keep product facts accurate and consistent with the product files.

## Commerce Copy

Shopping, account, and order messages follow the same voice: calm, clear, and brief. Name the candle, say what happened, and say what to do next. Never blame the customer and never use urgency (“Hurry!”, “Almost gone!”).

### Stock

| Situation | Copy |
|---|---|
| Sold out (button and card) | Sold out |
| Five or fewer left | Only a few left |
| Not enough stock at checkout | We have only [n] [candle name] left, so we've updated your bag. |
| Sold out at checkout | [Candle name] sold out while it was in your bag, so we've removed it. |

### Checkout

| Situation | Copy |
|---|---|
| Checkout button | Checkout |
| Shipping line, $35 or more | Complimentary |
| Shipping line, under $35 | $[rate] — complimentary on orders of $35 or more |
| Tax line | Sales tax ([rate]%) |
| Returned from a cancelled checkout | Your bag is just as you left it. |
| Checkout unavailable | Checkout is resting for a moment. Please try again shortly. |
| Confirmation heading | Thank you. Your candles are on their way to you soon. |
| Confirmation while payment is confirming | We're confirming your payment. This page will update in a moment. |
| Guest prompt after purchase | Create an account to keep track of this order and the next one. |

### Order Status (shown to customers)

| Status | Label | Line |
|---|---|---|
| `pending_payment` | Awaiting payment | We're waiting for your payment to be confirmed. |
| `paid` | Preparing | We're getting your candles ready to ship. |
| `fulfilled` | Shipped | Your candles are on their way. |
| `expired` | Not completed | This checkout wasn't completed, so nothing was charged. |
| `cancelled` | Cancelled | This order was cancelled and refunded in full. |
| `refunded` | Refunded | This order was refunded. |
| `partially_refunded` | Partly refunded | Part of this order was refunded. |

### Accounts

| Situation | Copy |
|---|---|
| Sign-in heading | Welcome back. |
| Sign-up heading | Create your account. |
| Sign-up lead | Keep your orders in one place. You never need an account to check out. |
| Account heading | Hello, [first name]. (or “Your account” without a name) |
| No orders yet | No orders yet — the season is just getting started. |
| Check-your-email | Check your inbox. We've sent a link to [email]. |
| Wrong credentials | That email and password don't match. Please try again. |
| Deletion requested | We've received your request and will confirm by email once your account is closed. |

### Authentication Emails

Subject lines are short and plain. Bodies are two sentences at most, followed by one button.

| Email | Subject | Body |
|---|---|---|
| Confirm sign-up | Confirm your NATH & JAID account | Welcome. Confirm your email to finish creating your account. |
| Magic link | Your NATH & JAID sign-in link | Here's your link to sign in. It works once and expires soon. |
| Reset password | Reset your NATH & JAID password | Use this link to choose a new password. If you didn't ask for this, you can ignore this email. |
| Change email | Confirm your new email address | Confirm this address to use it for your NATH & JAID account. |
